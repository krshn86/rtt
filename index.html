What is Rapid Transformational Therapy?

Rapid Transformational Therapy (RTT) is a one-of-a-kind method, developed by British hypnotherapist Marisa Peer. This method is certainly 'Rapid' and 'Transformational', because special results are achieved in a short time.

RTT can save you hours and in most cases even years of talk therapy and coaching. Through hypnosis you learn to work with your subconscious mind and to detect old blockages. In this way you can clear and transform that which no longer serves you.

RTT makes you super grounded – and at the same time, new avenues open up. You make new choices that deep down you know are right. Who do you want to be? RTT gives you back the freedom to choose for yourself.

This revolutionary, highly efficient and effective stand-alone therapy is a perfect fit for our fast-paced lifestyle and the rapidly changing world we live in today.

An RTT process has different phases, which together cover about four weeks. Afterwards, you look differently at yourself and at life in general.

01

Intake

A free 30-minute discovery call to get to know each other and formulate a clear goal.

02

Online, in the comfort of your home. Reserve 1.5–2 hours for this appointment.

Customised Audio

A personalised 15-minute audio recording — your new story, your new programme. Listen daily for 3 weeks.

04

Check-up

After a week we discuss how things are going. You can always reach out in between.

05

After three weeks we see what has permanently changed and what your life looks like now.

Session Content

What Happens During a Session

A session with RTT has three clear steps. Here is what the experience looks like from start to finish.

Step 1: Identify the Cause

After a short induction into hypnosis — a very deep relaxation — I guide you to identify the cause of your behaviour or problem. You are not sleeping, but very relaxed and aware.

Step 2: Interrupt the Pattern

Once we find the cause, you are immediately set free. With the powerful techniques of RTT I reprogram your brain with tailor-made positive commands.

Step 3: Install Positive Beliefs

The last part is a hypnotic transformation I record and give to you after the session, so you can listen to it at least once a day for the next 21 days.

Frequently Asked Questions

Still not sure? That's completely fine. Here are the things people usually ask before we begin.

Hypnosis is an open state of mind. It is a natural phenomenon, often described as a deep state of relaxation and concentration. During hypnosis you do not sleep and you are not unconscious. It is a positive state, also called a hypnotic state or state of creative relaxation.

During the session I gently guide you to a state of deep relaxation. Once you are completely relaxed, we focus on what you need help with. You let yourself be carried away with my flow of words, instructions and different mental exercises — specially designed to help you achieve positive change.

For nine out of ten clients, one session is enough to make a real shift. Some come back after a while — not because the first one didn't work, but because once you've experienced what's possible, you get curious about what else is there.

Traditional therapy often works with the conscious mind — talking through the same problem week after week. RTT goes directly to the subconscious, where the pattern actually lives. That's why it can save you, in many cases, years of talk therapy.
