What is Rapid Transformational Therapy?

Rapid Transformational Therapy (RTT) is a one-of-a-kind method, developed by British hypnotherapist Marisa Peer. This method is certainly 'Rapid' and 'Transformational', because special results are achieved in a short time. Not only in my practice, but worldwide.

RTT can save you hours and in most cases even years of talk therapy and coaching. Through hypnosis you learn to work with your subconscious mind and to detect old blockages. In this way you can clear and transform that which no longer serves you. You will experience more peace, strength, energy and space, in every aspect of your life.

RTT makes you super grounded – and at the same time, new avenues open up. You make new choices that deep down you know are right. Who do you want to be? RTT gives you back the freedom to choose for yourself.

This revolutionary, highly efficient and effective stand-alone therapy is a perfect fit for our fast-paced lifestyle and the rapidly changing world and demanding environments we live in today. It improves, transforms and optimizes the quality of life for people who do not have the time, desire or inclination to spend hours talking about their problem.

Method

The RTT Process

An RTT process has different phases, which together cover about four weeks. Afterwards, you look differently at yourself and at life in general.

01

Intake

It all starts with a free 30-minute discovery call. I will learn more about you and your problem, how it manifests, and above all what you would want to do differently. We formulate a clear and measurable goal — and make an appointment for the therapy session.

02

Therapy Session

The therapy session takes place online, within the comfort of your home. Reserve two hours (1.5 hours on average) for this appointment. Also take some time for yourself afterwards.

03

Customised Audio

Based on what emerged during the session, I create a 15-minute audio especially tailored for you. You listen to it every day for three weeks — because your mind learns by repetition.

04

Check-up

After a week we will be in touch to discuss how things are going. You can always send me a message if there is something you want to share or discuss in between.

05

Evaluation

After three weeks we discuss again and see what has permanently changed and what your life looks like now. Usually this final step marks the completion of the transformation process.

Session Content

What Happens During a Session

A session with RTT has three clear steps. Here is what the experience looks like from start to finish.

Step 1: Identify the Cause

After a short induction into hypnosis — a very deep relaxation — I guide you to identify the cause of your behaviour or problem. You are not sleeping, but very relaxed and aware. Your subconscious has stored every detail and every memory of your life, and this is how we find the cause.

Step 2: Interrupt the Pattern

Once we find the cause, you are immediately set free and let go of the old beliefs that kept you in this vicious circle. With the powerful techniques of RTT I give your subconscious an "update" and reprogram your brain with tailor-made positive commands.

Step 3: Install Positive Beliefs

The last part of the session is a hypnotic transformation that I record and give to you after the session, so that you can listen to it at least once a day for the next 21 days.

I'm Angela, I live in both France and The Netherlands, and I share this wonderful adventure with my dog Mya, the most amazing furry family.

I've always had an interest in psychology, and after 20 years spent in corporate environments, spinning on the same wheel with no finish line in sight, I felt it was time to change the narrative. My narrative. So, in the middle of a world pandemic I asked myself the most important question yet: "how can I drive my life towards the path I really want to follow?"

This is how I discovered Rapid Transformational Therapy (RTT). RTT is a combination of hypnosis, psychotherapy, neuro-linguistic programming (NLP) and cognitive behavioral therapy. The method is revolutionary because it breaks with various conventions, including the idea that change is always painful, lengthy and complicated. RTT proves that it can be done differently.

At the end of 2021 I successfully completed the RTT Practitioner training, obtained my certificate and I am now a therapist who can help others change their lives. I am grateful for everything I have encountered on my path. I am happy to help you build that bridge to a life with more abundance and freedom.
