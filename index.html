The Method

What is Rapid Transformational Therapy?

RTT® was developed by British hypnotherapist Marisa Peer, built on over 30 years of experience. It brings together the best of hypnotherapy, NLP, psychotherapy and cognitive behavioural therapy — and it works fast.

Most of what holds us back isn't conscious. It lives in patterns formed a long time ago — beliefs we picked up as children, stories we told ourselves to make sense of the world. RTT helps you find where those patterns began. And more importantly, change them.

In one session, you can discover how to convert the pain of the past into fuel for the life you want to build. Not months of appointments. Not years of talking about the same problem. One session — and a new story begins.

During a session, you enter a state of deep relaxation — not sleep, more like a very focused daydream. You remain fully aware and in control the whole time. We simply go somewhere your daily mind can't easily reach on its own.

How It Works

The RTT Process

From our first conversation to your final check-in — here's what working together actually looks like.

01

Discovery Call

A free 30-minute conversation to get to know each other. We look at what you want to change and whether RTT is the right fit.

02

Intake Form

A short intake so I can understand your situation properly before we begin — and personalise everything for you.

03

RTT Session

90–120 minutes, online, from the comfort of your home. We go deep — gently — and find what's ready to shift.

04

Your Personal Recording

After the session, I create a short audio tailored specifically to you. You listen to it daily for 21 days — that's how the new pattern takes root.

05

Check-in & Evaluation

We check in after one week, and again at three weeks, to see what's changed and what you're noticing.
