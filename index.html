Method

The RTT Process

An RTT process has different phases, which together cover about four weeks. Afterwards, you look differently at yourself and at life in general.

01

Intake

It all starts with a free 30-minute discovery call. We get to know each other. I will learn more about you and your problem, how it manifests, and above all what you would want to do differently. We formulate a clear and measurable goal — and make an appointment for the therapy session.

02

Therapy Session

The therapy session takes place online, within the comfort of your home. Reserve two hours (1.5 hours on average) for this appointment. Also take some time for yourself afterwards.

03

Customised Audio

Based on what emerged during the session, I create a 15-minute audio especially tailored for you — your new story, your new programme. You listen to it every day for three weeks, because your mind learns by repetition.

04

Check-up

After a week we will be in touch to discuss how things are going. You can always send me a message if there is something you want to share or discuss in between.

05

Evaluation

After three weeks we discuss again and see what has permanently changed and what your life looks like now. Usually this final step marks the completion of the transformation process. If anything extra is needed, we will discuss that as well.

Session Content

What Happens During a Session

A session with RTT has three clear steps. Here is what the experience looks like from start to finish.

Step 1: Identify the Cause

After a short induction into hypnosis — a very deep relaxation — I guide you to identify the cause of your behaviour or problem. You are not sleeping, but very relaxed and aware. Your subconscious has stored every detail and every memory of your life, and this is how we find the cause.

Step 2: Interrupt the Pattern

Once we find the cause, you are immediately set free and let go of the old beliefs that kept you in this vicious circle. With the powerful techniques of RTT I give your subconscious an "update" and reprogram your brain with tailor-made positive commands, stimulating the desired new behaviour.

Step 3: Install Positive Beliefs

The last part of the session is a hypnotic transformation that I record and give to you after the session, so that you can listen to it at least once a day for the next 21 days.

Frequently Asked Questions

Still not sure? That's completely fine. Here are the things people usually ask before we begin. Don't see yours? Just ask.

Hypnosis is an open state of mind. It is a natural phenomenon, often described as a deep state of relaxation and concentration. The mind distances itself from the daily worries and activities. During hypnosis you do not sleep and you are not unconscious. It is a changing state of consciousness, often referred to as a "trance" — a positive state, also called a hypnotic state or state of creative relaxation.

During the session I gently guide you to a state of deep relaxation. Once you are completely relaxed, we focus on what you need help with. You let yourself be carried away with my flow of words, instructions and different mental exercises — specially designed to help you achieve positive change.

Hypnosis works because it allows you to communicate directly with your subconscious mind — the invisible part of your mind that controls you every waking moment. Bypassing the conscious part of the mind is what makes hypnosis so effective. Once the subconscious accepts newer, better ideas and beliefs, they will be carried out through the body.

The vast majority of people are able to relax themselves deeply enough to enter a light trance and open access to the subconscious. If you are willing to listen while relaxing as deeply as possible, you will experience the benefits of hypnosis. I cannot predict exactly how it will work for you, because everyone is unique — and therefore your experience will be unique as well.

Most sessions last between 1.5 and 2 hours. Please reserve enough time in your agenda and allow yourself some quiet time after the session as well.

For nine out of ten clients, one session is enough to make a real shift. Some come back after a while — not because the first one didn't work, but because once you've experienced what's possible, you get curious about what else is there. During our discovery call I'll give you my honest read on what makes sense for you.

Traditional therapy often works with the conscious mind — talking through the same problem week after week. RTT goes directly to the subconscious, where the pattern actually lives. That's why it can save you, in many cases, years of talk therapy. We're not managing the symptoms. We're finding the cause and changing it.
